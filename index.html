Player Profile

Who is Ross?

"I'm not just building websites. I'm trying to build places where people feel like they belong."

I grew up in a small town in Vietnam, the kind of place where it rained more often than not. At sixteen, I packed a bag and moved to Japan with a head full of dreams I couldn't even put into words yet.

These days I'm a university student, a designer, and sometimes a storyteller too. I shoot film because I want to hold onto the small moments most people scroll right past. I write code because I want to make things that feel a little human. I design because I genuinely believe clarity is its own kind of kindness.

This is my story. It's nowhere near finished.

The chapters

From Vietnam to the world, six chapters of figuring things out

Chapter I

Homeland · Vietnam

I grew up under a sky that cried a lot. The monsoon taught me early on that beauty and intensity usually show up together. I learned to spot stories in ordinary things, an old man's smile, the hum of a motorbike passing by. Creativity, for me, started as just wanting to hold onto moments before they slipped away.

Chapter II

The Leap · Arrival in Japan

Sixteen years old, one backpack, and a head full of questions I couldn't have explained in any language. Loneliness felt like a blank page back then, and slowly I started filling it in, with new friends, new words, and this quiet realization that maybe I actually belonged somewhere I never expected to.

Chapter III

Awakening · High School Years

High school was where I figured out I could hold two whole identities at once. Calculus by day, writing stories nobody would ever read by night. I shot my first roll of film, built my first website, tiny little universes, but they were mine.

Chapter IV

Guardian · Cybersecurity Volunteer

For three years, I stood between everyday people and the ones trying to take advantage of them. Nobody made me do it. I just believed technology should protect people, not betray them. It taught me that engineering, at its core, is really just ethics you practice every day.

Chapter V

Scholar · University

University taught me design isn't about making things pretty. It's about making someone feel seen. I still walk these campus halls, notebooks full of ideas somewhere between code and poetry.

Chapter VI

Horizon · What's Next

I'm getting ready to take what I've learned out into the world, not as an expert, but as someone who still thinks curiosity is the most useful skill there is. New cultures, new people to work with, and a future where design doesn't really care about borders.
