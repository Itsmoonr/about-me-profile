Player Profile

Who is Ross?

“I don't just build websites. I build bridges between cultures, between silence and voice, between a blank canvas and a story that matters.”

I was born in Vietnam, grew up between rain and rice fields, and arrived in Japan at sixteen — alone, confused, and full of questions. Today, I'm a student at a Japanese university, learning to turn questions into art.

I shoot film to remember what it feels like to be a stranger. I write code to build places where others can feel at home. I design because I believe that beauty and clarity are forms of care.

This is my story. It's still being written.

The chapters

From Vietnam to the World — Six Chapters of Growth

Chapter I

Homeland · Vietnam

I was born under a sky that knew how to cry. The monsoon taught me early that beauty and intensity live side by side. I learned to see stories in the ordinary – an old man's smile, a motorbike's hum. Creativity started as a way to hold onto fleeting moments.

Chapter II

The Leap · Arrival in Japan

I arrived in Japan at sixteen with a backpack and a heart full of questions I couldn't articulate in any language. Loneliness became a canvas, and I painted it with new friendships, new words, and the quiet revelation that I could belong somewhere I'd never imagined.

Chapter III

Awakening · High School Years

High school was where I discovered that I could hold two identities at once. By day, I learned calculus. By night, I wrote stories no one would ever read. I shot my first roll of film, built my first website – tiny universes that felt truly mine.

Chapter IV

Guardian · Cybersecurity Volunteer

I spent three years standing between people and those who would exploit them. Not because I had to, but because I believe that technology should protect, not betray. It taught me that engineering is ethics in practice.

Chapter V

Scholar · University

University taught me that design isn't about making things beautiful. It's about making people feel seen. Now I walk the halls of a Japanese university, notebooks filled with ideas that blur the line between code and poetry.

Chapter VI

Horizon · The Next Frontier

I'm preparing to carry what I've learned to the world — not as an expert, but as a student who still believes that curiosity is the greatest skill. New cultures, new collaborations, and a future where design knows no borders.
