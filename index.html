The chapters

Chapter I

Homeland · Vietnam

Born into a world of rain and rice fields. I learned to see stories in the ordinary – an old man's smile, a motorbike's hum. Creativity started as a way to capture fleeting moments.

Chapter II

The Leap · Arrival in Japan

At sixteen, I stepped off the plane into a language I didn't speak and a city that never slept. Loneliness became a canvas, and I painted it with new friendships and discovery.

Chapter III

Awakening · High School Years

Between calculus and calligraphy club, I found my voice. I wrote my first short story, shot my first roll of film, and built my first website – tiny universes that felt truly mine.

Chapter IV

Guardian · Cybersecurity Volunteer

I volunteered to protect the digital spaces of those who couldn't protect themselves. It taught me that technology isn't just about innovation – it's about responsibility and care.

Chapter V

Scholar · University

Now, I walk the halls of a university in Japan, notebooks filled with ideas. I'm learning that design isn't about making things pretty – it's about understanding people, then making them feel seen.

Chapter VI

Horizon · The Next Frontier

After building my foundation in Japan, I'm preparing to take my craft to an English-speaking world. New cultures, new collaborations, and a future where design knows no borders.
