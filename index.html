Profile

Who is Ross?

"I build digital spaces that feel like home — even if you've never been there."

I grew up in a small town in Vietnam where it rained more often than not. At sixteen, I packed a bag and moved to Japan with a head full of dreams I couldn't yet put into words.

Today I'm a university student, a designer, and a storyteller. I shoot film to hold onto the small moments most people scroll past. I write code to make things that feel human. I design because clarity, I've learned, is its own kind of kindness.

This is my story — and it's still being written.

Story Mode

The chapters

From Vietnam to the world — six chapters of figuring things out

Chapter I

Homeland · Vietnam

I grew up under a sky that cried a lot. The monsoon taught me early that beauty and intensity travel together. I learned to spot stories in ordinary things — an old man's smile, the hum of a motorbike passing by. Creativity, for me, started as a way to hold onto moments before they slipped away.

Chapter II

The Leap · Arrival in Japan

Sixteen years old, one backpack, and a head full of questions I couldn't explain in any language. Loneliness felt like a blank page back then. Slowly, I started filling it in — with new friends, new words, and the quiet realization that maybe I belonged somewhere I never expected to.

Chapter III

Awakening · High School Years

High school was where I figured out I could hold two whole identities at once. Calculus by day, writing stories nobody would read by night. I shot my first roll of film, built my first website — tiny little universes, but they were mine.

Chapter IV

Guardian · Cybersecurity Volunteer

For three years, I stood between everyday people and the ones trying to take advantage of them. Nobody made me do it. I just believed technology should protect people, not betray them. It taught me that engineering, at its core, is ethics you practice every day.

Chapter V

Scholar · University

University taught me that design isn't about making things pretty — it's about making someone feel seen. I still walk these halls, notebooks full of ideas somewhere between code and poetry.

Chapter VI

Horizon · What's Next

I'm getting ready to take what I've learned out into the world — not as an expert, but as someone who still believes curiosity is the most useful skill there is. New cultures, new people to work with, and a future where design doesn't care about borders.
